# ADR 0005: Guided audience mapping and explicitly estimated strip

Status: accepted

Keep raw microphone measurements immutable. Add a browser-local choice to show
A-weighted audience estimates in the strip. The source is prominently labelled
EST AUDIENCE; missing mapping produces blanks, never silent raw fallback.
C peak and spectrum remain fixed-mic measurements. Existing alarm thresholds
remain fixed-mic thresholds and are described as such in the controls.

Mapping uses unchanged external pink-noise playback and five operator-initiated
captures: fixed position, three audience positions, fixed position again. Each
capture integrates 30 seconds of fresh A-weighted block energy on the host.
Reject gaps, clipping, calibration changes, replay sources and active events.
Reject more than 2 dB disagreement between fixed checks as an operational drift
check, not an accuracy specification. Compute the equal-energy average of the
three audience captures minus the equal-energy average of the two fixed checks.

The user names and applies the profile explicitly; original captures and input
identity are retained in settings/event metadata. Settings changes restart
averages and cannot occur during an event. The procedure samples three chosen
locations; it does not establish a room-wide or maximum exposure. Occupancy and
speaker/layout changes require reassessment. No synthesized audio is transmitted
or generated by the appliance. See microphone-placement.md for positioning.
